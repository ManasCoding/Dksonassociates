Caliber Tech Solutions is a global engineering company specialising in structural steel detailing, structural steel design, BIM modeling and material take-off.

For more than two decades we have worked as the detailing and engineering desk behind fabricators, EPC contractors and construction companies. Our teams model every member and connection, check every drawing twice, and release fabrication-ready packages that move straight to the shop floor.

We focus on three things clients measure us by: accuracy, fast turnaround and cost-effective delivery on complex steel projects across North America and beyond.

  • Tekla Structures and SDS/2 production teams
  • AISC & CISC detailing standards
  • CNC, DXF & E-sheet deliverables

How a project moves through Caliber

A predictable sequence, with checking built into every stage.

  1. 01

    Review & RFIs

    We study the contract drawings and raise RFIs early, before they become site problems.

  2. 02

    3D model

    Every member, plate and bolt is modeled and coordinated with other trades.

  3. 03

    Independent check

    A second detailer checks the model and drawings against the design intent.

  4. 04

    Approval

    Drawings go out for approval and comments are turned around quickly.

  5. 05

    Fabrication release

    Shop drawings, CNC files and reports are issued ready for production.
